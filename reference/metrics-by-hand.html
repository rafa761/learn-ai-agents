Metrics by hand

Every score is a fraction: numerator over denominator. Name the denominator before you believe the number. Holds the answer keys for lesson 0007's pencil work.

§1The fraction table

Recall and precision share a numerator and swap denominators. MRR divides by a position. Faithfulness divides by a claim count — never chunks, citations, or documents. Harness numbers are means of per-row fractions; the mean hides which row owes the debt, so decompose before you optimize.
